## 2026-04-08 - Cache DOM Queries in High Frequency Vanilla JS Game Loops\n**Learning:** Repeatedly querying the DOM with `document.getElementById` and `document.querySelector` inside `setInterval` loops (like a 500ms continuous damage tick) and rapid click event handlers causes unnecessary overhead and can throttle performance in vanilla clicker games. In this app, many core elements like the health bar, health texts, and sound files were re-queried on every single tick or click.\n**Action:** Always cache these frequently accessed DOM elements into global or scoped variables at the top level so they are looked up only once during initial load. This reduces CPU cycles spent on DOM traversal and allows for much smoother game loops and click handling.
