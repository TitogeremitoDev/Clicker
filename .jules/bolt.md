## 2024-06-19 - Vanilla JS Clicker Optimization\n**Learning:** In vanilla JS clicker games without frameworks, DOM lookups like `getElementById` in hot paths (click handlers or short `setIntervals`) can be significant performance bottlenecks, causing jank over time as the DOM grows.\n**Action:** Always cache these element references globally or in a closer scope before attaching listeners or intervals to prevent continuous DOM traversal overhead.
